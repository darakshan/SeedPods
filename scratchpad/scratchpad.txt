style/transitions=Style note for @section(transition). A transition is a seam sentence + a link. Simple shape: (1) One sentence naming what the current chapter leaves on the table — the loose thread, the question left open, the concept introduced that needs testing. Not a summary of what just happened. (2) @link(NNN) with one clause saying what the next chapter does with that thread. (3) Optional second link if the chapter genuinely hubs to more than one place. Avoid: recap sentences ("we saw that…"), formal-essay voice, restatement of the chapter's argument. Current working examples: 001-caloric ends with a longer argumentative bridge naming the hidden cost (phenomenon lost with mechanism), hands to 027. 039-devil is a hub fanning out to 027/021/070/035/069 with one-clause orientations each. 055-patience is compressed to two lines: "Patience builds the reflectors. @link(069) takes up what happens when two of them meet." Caveat noted 2026-07-03 by Darakshan: chapters are currently at very different completeness levels, so don't try to match a transition's density to the chapter's current polish — everything will be leveled in the final consistency pass. Until then, all transitions can follow the same simple shape and be rewritten later. Ordering (within each TOC section) is upstream of transitions and should probably be settled first.
