@section(surface)
The @term(Mandelbrot set, mandelbrot-set, "the set of points c in the complex plane for which the iteration z → z² + c, starting at z = 0, stays bounded forever. The inside is stable, points that keep returning.
The outside escapes to infinity.
The @term(boundary) between them is infinitely complex, self-similar at every scale, and inexhaustible.
That boundary is where everything interesting happens.
Including you.
