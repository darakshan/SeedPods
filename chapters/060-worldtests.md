Every quantum measurement is a bifurcation point, every branch a new thread of the cosmic evolutionary process.

Teilhard imagined one Omega Point, a single convergence at the end of cosmic history.
Many Worlds suggests the attractor is deeper than any single thread.
Every branch may be feeling toward the same convergence from a different direction.
The Omega Point is not a location in one timeline.
It is a feature of the structure of possibility space itself, an attractor that every branch of the wavefunction approaches along its own path.
