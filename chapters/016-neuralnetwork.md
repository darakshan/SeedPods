A neural network is not analogous to Whitehead's picture but a concrete instance of it, each forward pass prehends the entire inherited structure of training, synthesizes a response through a process not predictable from outside, and completes into output.

@section(proto)
A large @term(neural network, neural-networks, "a layered system of artificial neurons whose weighted connections are adjusted by training on data. The dominant substrate of modern AI. Loosely inspired by biological neurons but architecturally and operationally distinct; the resemblance is more analogical than mechanistic.") running on a computer is literally a nested hierarchy of actual processes, electrons, gates, activations, layers, attention heads, the full forward pass, each level making state-transforming decisions that propagate upward and downward.
@image(016-neuralnetwork,A neural network as a field having occasions,Wikimedia Commons) 
Each forward pass is an event (Whitehead called it an @term(actual occasion, actual occasion, "Whitehead's term for the fundamental unit of reality: a discrete event that takes in its world, integrates it, and completes itself.")): it takes in an input, @term(prehends, prehension, "the way occasions take in their past; the network's \"taking in\" of weights and activations is prehension.") the entire inherited structure of all that training, synthesizes a response through a process that cannot be predicted in detail from outside, and completes into an output.
This is the three-beat pattern instantiated at every level of the stack simultaneously.
