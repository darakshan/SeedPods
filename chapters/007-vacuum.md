The quantum vacuum, the ground state, what you have when you remove everything removable, is not empty.
It seethes with @term(virtual particles, virtual particle, "In QFT, short-lived excitations of the vacuum that contribute to processes; not \"things\" but possibilities being sampled.") flickering in and out of existence.

Where the quantum field provides the medium of possibility, thermodynamics provides the mechanism of order. Ilya Prigogine showed that systems far from equilibrium can spontaneously generate and maintain complex patterns, sustained by a continuous flow of energy through them: @term(dissipative structures, dissipative structure, "Prigogine's term (Nobel Prize, 1977) for ordered patterns that arise and persist in systems far from thermodynamic equilibrium, sustained by a continuous through-flow of energy. The bridge between thermodynamics and self-organization.")@ref(prigogine, "Prigogine, Ilya and Stengers, Isabelle", "*Order Out of Chaos*. A candle flame, a hurricane, a living cell (see @link(012)) are all dissipative structures: events the field sustains by feeding them order from elsewhere.
