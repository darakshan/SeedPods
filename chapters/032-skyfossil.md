Stars as moments in the thread.
The telos isn't survival.
It's self-awareness, the universe developing the capacity to know what it is.
@term(Many Worlds, many-worlds, "Hugh Everett's 1957 interpretation of quantum mechanics in which the wavefunction never collapses. The cost is an explosion of unobservable branches; the benefit is a strictly unitary, deterministic theory.")@ref(everett, "Everett, Hugh", "*Relative State Formulation of Quantum Mechanics*.
